Privacy policy

Last updated 9 September 2026. Applies to version 1.6.

X Bookmarks to Markdown is a browser extension that exports your X (formerly Twitter) bookmarks as Markdown files. It runs entirely on your own machine.

No data is collected

  • The extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, browsing history, or usage information.
  • There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking of any kind.
  • The extension makes no network requests of its own and talks to no server, API, or third party.

How your bookmarks are handled

  • Bookmark data is read from the X page that is already open in your browser and processed in memory.
  • The generated Markdown files are packed into a ZIP and handed straight to your browser's download prompt.
  • Nothing is kept afterwards: the extension stores no bookmark data, and closing the tab discards everything.
  • The exported files belong to you and are saved only where you choose to save them.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose and limits
activeTab Grants access to the tab you are viewing, and only after you click the extension icon. Access ends when you leave the tab.
scripting Injects the reader script into the bookmarks page so it can collect the posts displayed there. It runs on the active tab only, on x.com/i/bookmarks and x.com/i/history.
downloads Saves the generated ZIP file through the browser's download function. Your download history and existing files are never read.

Third parties

The extension bundles the open-source JSZip library to build the ZIP file locally. It integrates with no external service, shows no advertising, and shares nothing with anyone.

Verifying these claims

The complete source code is published under the MIT licence, so you do not have to take our word for it. Read the repository, or watch the network panel in your browser's developer tools during an export and confirm that no request leaves your machine.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices ever change, this page changes with it, and the edit is visible in the repository's history.
